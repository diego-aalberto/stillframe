# The Light We Leave On

A conversation-driven romantic ensemble story about becoming someone people expect to see tomorrow.

**Status:** playable illustrated game connecting the native comic engine to the local inference service. Four romanceable adult women, individual romance endings, and a consensual all-four ending. Each woman has 12 illustrated poses and 120 skeletal motion variants, with ten painted locations, a start menu, scene introductions, history and autosave.

- [Story bible](STORY_BIBLE.md): premise, personalities, relationship rules, and visual direction.
- [Scene graph](SCENE_GRAPH.md): playable nodes, conversation outcomes, route intersections, and endings.
- [LaTeX diagram](story-map.tex): shared progression, personal routes, and the all-four relationship path.
- [Play and implementation guide](../Game/README.md): launch, saves, character memory, and checks.

The player speaks freely. The listed outcomes are writing rules for interpreting conversation, not buttons or prescribed dialogue. A playthrough visits a subset of the scenes; it does not need to exhaust every character's path.

Run `../start-game.cmd`. The opening is `S01`, the rainy car ride with Hana. Speak freely; after shared scenes, choose personal visits. Four visits per window make it possible to develop all four romances. Kisses and cuddling are part of romantic scenes, with mutual interest and consent.
